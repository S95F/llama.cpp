# EXP-0003 - Raise the sampled-slot count from 16 to 32

**Status: complete. Decision: Adopt.** Iteration 3, authorized 2026-10-26.
Paused mid-iteration at the game check and resumed on `go`.

Roadmap item **R6**, the cheapest remaining item: its gain was already screened
by EXP-0002 before this iteration began.

## Hypothesis

**H1** - sampling 32 slots instead of 16 for the eviction victim is a free gain
below the crossover, and the hardware gain matches the simulated +0.39 to +0.87
within the 0.7 to 0.8 point RNG margin.

**Falsified by** the hardware gain falling outside that margin, by the paired
delta not being positive at every sub-crossover size, or by a measurable cost in
the eviction path.

**H2** - the change stays inert at and above the crossover, since `n_sample` is
only set in the sub-crossover branch.

## Motivation

EXP-0002 left the sample count at 16 deliberately, to keep one hypothesis per
change, and recorded that `dist s32` beat `dist s16` by +0.39 to +0.87
sign-consistently. This iteration spends that.

## Choosing the count: the curve saturates near 48

Rather than assume 32, this iteration screened 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 96 on
the four EXP-0001 traces. Mean hit rate over four traces, raw data in
`knee_prompt*.json`:

| MiB | s8 | s16 | s24 | s32 | s48 | s64 | s96 | LFU | Belady |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 16.91 | 18.68 | 19.24 | 19.55 | 19.84 | 19.84 | 19.83 | 16.57 | 32.01 |
| 384 | 24.63 | 26.21 | 26.71 | 26.92 | 27.10 | 27.16 | 27.14 | 22.53 | 43.51 |
| 528 | 31.12 | 32.33 | 32.64 | 32.84 | 32.92 | 33.03 | 32.94 | 27.69 | 51.65 |
| 586 | 33.24 | 34.29 | 34.54 | 34.68 | 34.78 | 34.80 | 34.78 | 29.45 | 54.07 |

Marginal gain per doubling, averaged over sizes and traces:

| step | gain |
|---|---:|
| s8 -> s16 | +1.40 |
| s16 -> s32 | +0.62 |
| s32 -> s64 | +0.21 |
| s64 -> s96 | **-0.04** |

**32 was chosen over 48 or 64**, which measure better (+0.79 and +0.83 average
against +0.62): 32 takes three quarters of the available gain for half the
sampling work, and `AGENTS.md` prefers the simpler change that does most of the
job. The saturation curve is recorded so the choice can be revisited; it is a
one-constant change either way.

Note that sampling nearly the whole cache (s64, s96) still beats exact LFU by
over 3 points at 256 MiB (19.84 against 16.57). That is the distance term of
EXP-0002 doing the work, not sampling breadth, and it is why raising the count
does not drift toward the full-LFU behaviour that FAILED_APPROACHES.md rejects.

### Slots per cache group, which explains the shape

| MiB | group 0 (36 layers) | group 1 (1 layer) | group 2 (3 layers) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 124 | 2 | 9 |
| 384 | 187 | 4 | 14 |
| 528 | 258 | 6 | 20 |
| 586 | 286 | 6 | 22 |
| 645 | 315 | 7 | 25 |
| 1024 | 502 | 12 | 40 |
| 2048 | 1005 | 26 | 82 |

Group 1 never reaches `n_expert_used = 8` below the crossover, which is why it is
uncached and why layer 1 is missing from EXP-0002's per-layer table. Group 2 has
only 9 to 22 slots there, so sampling 16 or 32 of them **oversamples with
repeats**.

**The default is deliberately not clamped to `n_slots`.** Oversampling a tiny
group raises coverage rather than wasting work: 16 draws with replacement from 9
slots cover about 83% of them against 63% for 9 draws. Clamping would have been
the obvious "optimization" and would have made the policy slightly worse.

### The base changed mid-iteration

The user merged upstream master during this iteration, moving the base from
`d854d98b` to `a4699400e` (33 commits). One of them,
`a4699400e llama : move no_alloc out of llama_hparams (#31141)`, moved `no_alloc`
off `llama_hparams` and threaded it through the `llama_moe_cache` constructor.

The merge updated the signature and the callers but missed the host-pinning line
this branch added in `c71dee331`, which still read `model.hparams.no_alloc`. The
file therefore stopped compiling **as soon as it was touched** - the stale `.obj`
had hidden it. Fixed in `83ffbea00` by using the constructor argument already in
scope, which is how the buffer allocation a few lines above does it.

### The pause

executable under `steamapps`, not an idle launcher. Free host RAM had fallen to

On `go` the game check was overridden - but by then Stellaris had exited on its

## Results

### H1 confirmed: +0.53 to +0.86 points on hardware

255 greedy tokens on `moe-bench/prompt.txt`, from the cache's own counters:

| MiB | s16 | **32 (new default)** | gain | PCIe traffic | simulated gain |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 16.36% | **17.00%** | **+0.64** | **-0.76%** | +0.82 |
| 384 | 23.17% | **24.03%** | **+0.86** | **-1.12%** | +0.68 |
| 528 | 29.30% | **29.96%** | **+0.66** | **-0.93%** | +0.58 |
| 586 | 31.38% | **31.91%** | **+0.53** | **-0.78%** | +0.55 |

Hardware against simulator: -0.18, +0.18, +0.08, -0.02. Well inside the 0.7 to
0.8 point RNG margin, and the means agree almost exactly (+0.67 measured against
+0.66 simulated).

### H2 confirmed, and the base did not move the router

The `default` arm at 1024 and 2048 MiB, where nothing samples, reproduces the
figures recorded in two earlier sessions on the **previous** base **exactly**:

| MiB | EXP-0003 default | recorded (EXP-0001 / EXP-0002, base `d854d98b`) | |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1024 | 41.36% / 87882.56 MiB | 41.36% / 87882.56 MiB | **exact** |
| 2048 | 61.38% / 58033.62 MiB | 61.38% / 58033.62 MiB | **exact** |

Two conclusions in one table. The change is inert above the crossover, as H2
predicted. And **upstream's 33 new commits did not perturb the router's top-8**,
so the four committed traces and every simulated figure resting on them remain
valid for `a4699400e`. That resolves the caveat STATE.md raised when the base
moved - the re-collection EXP-0001 had to perform is **not** needed this time.

### A confound in the A/B toggle, measured rather than assumed

The `LLAMA_MOE_CACHE_SAMPLE` override clamps to `g.n_slots` while the automatic
path does not. So the `s16` arm is not exactly the old default on the small
group: at 256 MiB group 2 has 9 slots, so the env override gives 9 rather than
16, and at 384 MiB it gives 14.

Quantified against EXP-0002's recorded default arm, which was a true unclamped
16:

| MiB | EXP-0002 default (unclamped 16) | EXP-0003 s16 arm | delta | group 2 slots |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 16.34% | 16.36% | **+0.02** | 9 |
| 384 | 23.16% | 23.17% | **+0.01** | 14 |
| 528 | 29.30% | 29.30% | **0.00** | 20 |
| 586 | 31.38% | 31.38% | **0.00** | 22 |

At 528 and 586 MiB there is no clamping (16 < 20, 22) and the arm reproduces
EXP-0002 **exactly**. At 256 and 384 the confound is +0.01 to +0.02 points, so it
changes no conclusion. Group 2 is 3 of 40 layers, and group 0 - where 36 of 40
layers and neither arm's count is clamped - carries the effect.

The simulator has no such asymmetry and predicted the same gain, which is the
independent check that the confound did not matter.

### Incidental: the first hardware measurement for Q10

Because the env override also defeats the crossover switch, the `s16` arm at 1024
and 2048 MiB is sampling-plus-distance **forced on above the crossover** - which
is exactly what OPEN_QUESTIONS.md Q10 asks about, and had only ever been
simulated:

| MiB | forced s16 + dist | inert (shipping) | delta | EXP-0002 simulated |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1024 | 43.46% | 41.36% | **+2.10** | +2.12 |
| 2048 | 61.23% | 61.38% | **-0.15** | -0.36 |

The 1024 MiB row matches the simulator to **0.02 points**. So the above-crossover
behaviour EXP-0002 declined to ship is real on hardware, and Q10 is worth the
traces it needs. Recorded there.

### Eviction path cost

Not measurable here and not claimed either way. 32 samples is twice the work per
fill and raises the draw cap from 64 to 128, against roughly 360 microseconds for
each 1.812 MiB upload the fill accompanies. Nothing in the hit-rate data suggests
a cost, but throughput could not resolve one (EXP-0002's control row moved 6.9%
at comparable free RAM), so **no throughput measurement was attempted this
iteration.**

## Conclusions

**Decision: Adopt.**

- **H1 confirmed:** +0.53 to +0.86 points of hit rate and 0.76 to 1.12% less
  PCIe traffic, matching the simulated prediction within 0.18 points.
- **H2 confirmed:** byte-identical above the crossover, verified against two
  earlier sessions' recorded figures.
- Correctness 8 of 8, on a new base.
- The change is **one constant**.

### Limitations

1. **Throughput never measured** for EXP-0002 or EXP-0003. The whole case for
   both rests on hit rate and uploaded bytes, which are exact. On this host the
   control row moves several percent, so a 1 to 3% effect is not resolvable.
2. **32 is not the optimum**, 48 to 64 is. +0.21 points were left deliberately
   on the table for half the sampling work.
3. **The eviction path cost is argued, not measured.**
4. **One model, one GPU, four prompts**, unchanged throughout. The small group's
   oversampling behaviour in particular is a property of this model's three-group
   `UD` quantization.
5. The A/B toggle's clamp asymmetry, bounded above at 0.02 points.
